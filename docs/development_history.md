# Development history

The timeline below is based only on retained local directories and reports.

1. **Public Baseline** — initial public and portal collection, source extraction, and knowledge evidence.
2. **Portal exploration** — portal-specific material remained alongside the baseline because its crawler and evidence are coupled.
3. **Public Expansion V1/V2** — iterative expansion followed by audited/canonical V2 outputs.
4. **Human annotation** — review states and human-audit workbooks were assembled from staging and restricted candidates.
5. **Retrieval / RAG** — v0 and v1 retrieval/RAG experiments and model-index artifacts.
6. **Public Staging** — frozen public candidate corpus used by downstream stages.
7. **Restricted Expansion V1** — planning, safety/quality gates, audit, candidates, extracted content, and final reports.
8. **Current stage** — a collaborative repository with frozen evidence retained, experimental evaluations separated, and next work starting from review status and stage READMEs.

No undocumented experiment has been added to this timeline.
