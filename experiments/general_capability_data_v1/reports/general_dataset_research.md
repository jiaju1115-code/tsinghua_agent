# General dataset research

Official dataset pages were rechecked online. OASST1 is retained as metadata-only pending sample-level filtering and revision pinning. GSM8K, MMLU and AI2 ARC are recorded as evaluation-only because they are benchmark datasets. OpenOrca is excluded for scale and synthetic-provenance risk. No external rows were downloaded.

Sources: [OASST1](https://huggingface.co/datasets/OpenAssistant/oasst1), [GSM8K](https://huggingface.co/datasets/openai/gsm8k), [MMLU](https://huggingface.co/datasets/cais/mmlu/tree/main), [AI2 ARC](https://huggingface.co/datasets/allenai/ai2_arc/tree/main).
