# PARTIAL Gold Policy V1

A Gold `PARTIAL` is permitted only for a query with at least two independent required points, where a locatable, conflict-free evidence span supports at least one point and at least one other point is independently missing or insufficient. The supported points must form a safe sub-answer.

A single incomplete point, wrong attribute/object, entity/scope/numeric/temporal/negation/logical conflict, OOD overlap, or ambiguous multi-object match is `NOT_SUPPORTED`, not `PARTIAL`. This is a dataset/evaluation policy and does not patch production Evidence.
