Housing

On-campus Accommodation



International Student Apartments of Tsinghua University was established in 2004. It is located in the northeast corner of the campus, close to the northeast gate of the university (Zijing Gate). The on-campus apartments include Zijing Student Apartment Buildings No. 19-23 and Student Apartment Building No.6 (Asian Youth Center). Full-time international students with university status and studying at the main campus are eligible to apply for accommodation in the International Student Apartments of Tsinghua University.

In recent years, the university has been renovating existing apartments year by year to provide on-campus accommodation resources for more international students. Currently, the international student apartments can accommodate more than 3,000 students. The main responsibilities of the apartment include consulting and handling international students' check-in, check-out, and accommodation information registration; managing daily safety and hygiene in the apartment building; organizing student community cultural activities; and working with students to build a safe and warm student community. In addition, the apartment has a work-study team - the International Dormitory Representatives. As a bridge between students and apartments, they assist apartments in carrying out safety and health inspections, cultural construction, and other related matters to improve student accommodation environment and enrich the culture in student apartment buildings. We welcome you to join this passionate and energetic group!

The International Student Apartment provides two types of dormitories: double room (two beds in one room, shared bathroom on each floor), triple room (three beds in one room, shared bathroom on each floor). In-room facilities mainly include air conditioning, Internet, bedding and furniture. Public area facilities mainly include washing machines, dryers, study rooms, light sports spaces, etc.

Students who are eligible to move in shall have legal and valid identity documents that are consistent with the university's admission status when checking in and registering for accommodation information. During their stay, students should abide by Chinese laws and regulations, and abide by the "Administrative Rules on Disciplinary Sanctions for Student Misconduct of Tsinghua University", "Administrative Measures for Accommodation in Student Apartments at Tsinghua University" and other relevant rules and regulations of the university and apartments.


To learn more about the on-campus international student apartments, please contact the general service desk of International Student Apartments.

Off-campus Accommodation

Due to limited amount of accommodation available on campus, some students will need to live off-campus and arrange accommodation in advance. 


When you live off-campus, never forget to register at your local police station to obtain a Registration Form of Temporary Residence within 24 hours after your arrival in China or signing of the rental contract.


The Registration Form of Temporary Residence is another important certificate for your legal stay in China, aside from your passport and visa. Therefore, a special reminder is that before you sign the contract or pay for the rental, do confirm that the owner of the apartment/house property is able to process the


accommodation registration for you. Otherwise you will get into trouble of illegal stay. Moreover, you need to renew your Registration Form of Temporary Residence if you have moved to another place, changed your passport or renewed your visa.
