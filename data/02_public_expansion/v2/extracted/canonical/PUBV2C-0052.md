According to China  Scholarship  Council regulations, if the host university permits  students  to live off campus on the Chinese Mainland, the university will provide a monthly/quarterly accommodation allowance.

**Application   Procedure**

1. Please complete the【To  be  completed  by applicant】section of the Tsinghua University Chinese Government Scholarship Dormitory Refund Application Form and send the form with your signature via email to offcampusliving@tsinghua.edu.cn   and CC gaopei@tsinghua.edu.cn.

2. Please complete check-out procedures at the General  Service Desk of Zijing Building 23.

3. The ISSC  will  review your Application Form  and  confirm the move-out date with the General Service Desk.The subsidy will be transferred to the  Bank of China  account  connected to your student ID card.
